Cricket Legends: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026. Made by Banx Gamex (solo developer Aaron Banks).

The short version

Cricket Legends collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your saves

Your careers, players, settings and progress are stored only on your own device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in a browser) deletes them.

The Full Game Unlock

The one optional purchase, the Full Game Unlock, is handled entirely by your app store (Google Play or the App Store) under that store's own privacy policy. The game only receives a yes-or-no answer about whether the unlock has been bought, so it can open the full game. It never sees your payment details.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Contact Banx Gamex at the email address shown on the game's store page.
